About Otaku Overload & 24/7 Neko FM

Welcome to Otaku Overload, home of 24/7 Neko FM — an interactive anime radio lounge, community setlist voting platform, and AI-powered game audio workstation built for otaku culture fans, indie game developers, and synth lovers.

Platform Mission: To bridge community fan participation with cutting-edge audio tools — giving creators free access to high-fidelity AI music generation, game sound design assets, live streaming, and interactive discussion rooms.

Key Platform Features

📻 24/7 Live Radio Deck

Curated anime soundtrack streams, J-Rock powerplay, Vocaloid hits, and City Pop loops powered by real-time audio fingerprinting and frequency visualizers.

📋 Setlist Polls & Prep Room B

Vote hourly on upcoming song covers performed by Girls Dead Phantom (Akari Minase, Nekomi, Sera, and Aoi) and submit custom song cover requests.

✨ AI Music & Game Audio Studio

Render multi-instrumental listening tracks, 16-bit chiptunes, RPG boss themes, and seamless background audio loops tailored for GDevelop, RPG Maker, and Roblox Studio.

💬 Live Community & Podcasts

Participate in real-time chatrooms, join age-verified channel rooms, customize avatar cosmetics, and comment on episode podcasts with community members.

Virtual Economy & Orbs System

Otaku Overload operates an in-app utility credit system called Orbs. Users receive initial credits upon signing up and can earn additional Orbs through daily check-ins or by watching optional sponsor video ads ("Watch for Orbs"). Orbs are used to power AI track rendering durations inside the music workstation.

Game Developer & Creative Toolset

In addition to web listening, our AI Studio provides game developers with real-time Web Audio DSP equalizer controls (Bass, Mid, High, Reverb), 0.5-second seamless looper crossfading, browser stem separation (Bass/Drums & Synth/Guitar tracks), and JSON metadata exports for game project integration.
