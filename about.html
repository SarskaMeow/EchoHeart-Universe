About Otaku Overload

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to Otaku Overload, home of 24/7 Neko FM — an immersive anime radio lounge, community setlist voting platform, and AI-powered game audio workstation built for otaku culture fans, indie game developers, and synthwave lovers.

What Otaku Overload Does: We bridge fan community participation with cutting-edge browser tools. Our platform provides live audio streams, interactive chatrooms, podcast comment feeds, setlist polls for Girls Dead Phantom, and an AI Music Studio for rendering custom game soundtracks.

Core Platform Features

📻 24/7 Neko FM Radio Deck

Continuous J-Rock powerplays, Vocaloid hits, and synth streams with real-time frequency visualizers.

🎙️ Whisper Radio Podcasts

Full-length audio drama episodes featuring Nekomi, Sera, Akari Minase, and Aoi with live listener comment discussions.

✨ AI Audio Workstation

Render multi-instrumental tracks, chiptunes, and loops using browser AI tools, complete with Web Audio DSP equalizers and stem exports.

💬 Sakuraoka Community BBS

Live chatrooms, profile cosmetics, investigator badge dossiers, and interactive voting poles.

Virtual Economy & Credits

Otaku Overload utilizes an in-app utility credit system called Orbs. Users earn Orbs through daily platform check-ins or by watching optional rewarded sponsor video ads, which power audio generation tasks within the workstation.
