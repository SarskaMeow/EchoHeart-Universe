Project Metadata

Series Title: EchoHeart (Volume 1)

Genre: Light Novel / Magical Girl / Music & Performance / Urban Fantasy / Sapphic (GL) Slow-Burn Romance / High School Slice-of-Life Drama

Setting: EchoHeart Senior Academy / Sakuraoka High School (Elite Class 3-A, Music Division), Tokyo, Japan. Homeroom Teacher: Harusawa-sensei.

Primary Bases of Operation

1. Music Prep Room B ("The Sanctuary"): Abandoned West Wing room used for club registration, evidence board tracking, and quiet rehearsals.

2. The Sub-Stage ("The Underground Arena"): A sprawling, secret subterranean concrete amphitheater forty feet beneath the West Wing. Features vaulted stone arches, acoustic walls, amber fairy lights, and vintage stage lighting.

Dual Persona System

The "Official" Persona (Light Music Research Group): Polite, tamed, low-volume acoustic pop or soft instrumental arrangements to keep conservative faculty members satisfied and avoid club suspension.

The "Underground" Persona (The Phantom Club / Girls of Phantom): High-decibel, heavily distorted 180 BPM driving J-Rock, J-Pop, and K-Pop hybrid tracks played exclusively in the Sub-Stage or during secret guerrilla performances.

Accumulated human grief, chronic stress, severe imposter syndrome, athletic/creative burnout, and unspoken loneliness. Appears as freezing drafts, static shockwaves, suffocating atmospheric pressure, gray smog, or frozen chains.

Strict Prose, Typography & Quality Control Engine

Typography & Font Sizing Restrictions

1. Regular Story Prose: MUST ALWAYS be standard, unbolded plain text (no bolding) and normal base font size. NEVER use Markdown headings or inline bolding for standard story sentences or dialogue.

2. Section & Scene Titles: MUST ALWAYS be sized up (using Markdown headings) and bolded with decorative symbols (❀, ⚡, ♪, ♡, ✦).

The Single-Line Formatting Rule

1. Line-by-Line Separation: Every single sentence or short dialogue phrase MUST be separated by a full empty line break (double return).

2. No Paragraph Clumping: Never group 3 or 4 sentences into a standard block paragraph. Keep prose formatted as clean, distinct single lines.

3. Double Space Around Headers & SFX: Always place an empty line before AND after section headings, scene titles, and sound effect markers.

Quality Control Engine (AI-Tell Filters)

❌ NO Explanatory Repetition (e.g., do not re-explain established lore every chapter).

❌ NO Structural Melodrama ("It was not a X, but a Y"). State sensory impact directly.

❌ NO Repetitive Sentence Openers (vary sentence rhythm naturally).

❌ NO Generic Emotion Labels ("felt sad", "was flustered"). Show physical reactions.

❌ NO Em Dashes (—). Replace with commas, ellipses, or new single lines.

❌ NO Clinical Jargon or Hyper-Technical Terms.
