Project Overview & World Lore

Overview

Series Title: EchoHeart (Girls of Phantom Series)

Genre: Light Novel / Urban Fantasy / Magical Girl / Music Performance / Sapphic (GL) Romance / Academy Life Drama

Setting: EchoHeart Senior Academy / Sakuraoka High School (Class 3-A, Music Division), Tokyo, Japan.

Bases of Operation

1. Music Prep Room B ("The Sanctuary"): Abandoned West Wing room used for official club registration, evidence board tracking, and confidential acoustic rehearsals.

2. The Sub-Stage ("The Underground Arena"): A secret subterranean concrete amphitheater forty feet beneath the West Wing. Features vaulted stone arches, acoustic damping walls, amber fairy lights, and vintage stage lighting.

Dual Persona System

The "Official" Persona (Light Music Research Group): Polite, low-volume acoustic pop and soft instrumental arrangements to keep faculty members satisfied and avoid club suspension.

The "Underground" Persona (Girls of Phantom / Phantom Club): High-decibel, heavily distorted 180 BPM driving J-Rock and K-Pop hybrid tracks played exclusively in the Sub-Stage or during secret guerrilla performances.

Investigation & Live Performance Workflow
1. Dissonance Radar
Detects sudden atmospheric temperature drops and localizes students drowning in emotional static.
2. Tactical Containment
Maps target schedules, secures acoustic permits, and builds green chalkboard profiles.
3. Delivery & Tickets
Intercepts targets or slips cardstock tickets into shoe lockers to bring listeners to the Sub-Stage.
4. Frontline Performance
Leads the musical attack, shreds high-gain J-Rock solos, and shatters Dissonance cores.

System & World Mechanics

Dissonance (Emotional Static)
Accumulated human grief, stress, imposter syndrome, and loneliness. Manifests as freezing drafts, static shockwaves, atmospheric pressure, gray smog, or frozen chains.
Resonance (Musical Frequency)
Pure emotional energy generated when playing weaponized instruments with genuine intent. Forms a color-coded 4-element barrier (Sanctuary Domain) that insulates targets and shatters Dissonance.
Heart Sync
Tracks emotional and musical alignment among the four members (Low → Building → Resonant → Harmonized → Overdrive).
Magical Pressure & Familiar Awareness
Monitors spirit entity presence and surrounding atmospheric strain (Calm → Detected → Surging → Dissonant Breach).

Sound & Atmospheric Textures

Heart Sync Pulses: Internal emotional flutters and resonance surges felt by the band during high-tension performances.

Resonance Barriers: Multi-color acoustic fields (Violet, Rose-Gold, Crimson, Teal) that insulate students from emotional static corruption.
