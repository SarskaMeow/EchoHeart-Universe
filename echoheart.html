🏫 Section 1: Inside Sakuraoka Senior Academy

Academic Hub

Classroom 3-A (Music Division)

📷 [IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: assets/class3a-sunset.jpg]

Location: Main Building, 3rd Floor West Row.

On the surface, Class 3-A serves as the rigorous academic pressure cooker where the girls maintain their spotless student records and innocent civilian cover. Beneath the veneer of chalkboard equations and attendance sheets, it is an arena of quiet tension. This is where Nekomi sits by the tall windows, pretending to read sheet music while secretly tracking the way Sera’s hair catches the golden afternoon light.

Sera frequently engineers small pretexts—such as dropping her erasers near Nekomi’s desk—just to spark a flustered, whispered exchange under the cover of classroom chatter. Meanwhile, Akari throws paper airplane notes across the aisles to test Aoi's ironclad patience, while Aoi quietly cross-references student absence logs against localized emotional static anomalies before the afternoon bell rings.

Operational Ground

Corridors & Shoe Locker Bays (Getabako)

📷 [IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: assets/lockers-morning.jpg]

The primary entrance atrium where everyday school routine collides with covert magical operations. The Getabako (shoe locker bays) act as the frontline drop zone for the band's critical Ticket Slip Interceptions. Guided by Aoi's tactical spreadsheets, Sera and Nekomi navigate the crowded morning rush to slide thick cardstock concert tickets into the shoe lockers of students suffering from severe Dissonance buildup.

These early morning hallway encounters are fraught with private emotion; it is during these quiet, empty corridor moments between homeroom periods that Nekomi's cool rockstar exterior completely dissolves if Sera accidentally brushes hands with her while placing a ticket into a compartment.

The Sanctuary

Music Prep Room B ("The Sanctuary")

📷 [IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: assets/preproom-twilight.jpg]

An officially abandoned practice space on the West Wing second floor that serves as the true emotional nucleus and safe haven for the band. Smelling faintly of old wood varnish, polished keytar plastic, and warm vacuum tubes, the room features an evidence chalkboard plastered with student Dissonance reports connected by red yarn.

When heavy rain beats against the window panes, Akari bangs out aggressive rhythm patterns on practice pads to blow off steam, while their familiar spirits—Byakko, Yozora, Kagerou, and Komorebi—rest lazily on the amplifier stacks. Most importantly, behind Locker 102 lies a concealed mechanical latch and acoustic resonance seam that unlocks directly down into the subterranean caverns below.

🌳 Section 2: School Grounds & Underground Secrets

Campus Boundary

The Front Gate & Cherry Blossom Courtyard

📷 [IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: assets/sakuraoka-gate.jpg]

The iconic brick-and-stone entrance gates of Sakuraoka High, framed by blooming sakura trees and boundary walls bearing the official academy plaque (桜岡高校). This threshold marks the boundary line where public civilian rules clash with private magical awakenings. During the morning commute under falling pink petals, the four girls walk through the gates together, putting on their polite public masks for the rest of the student body.

However, the courtyard bicycle racks and stone pathways are also where they first detect students experiencing acute emotional static—often marked by students lingering too long in isolation, staring blankly into space as invisible frost begins to freeze their daily steps.

Underground Arena

The Sub-Stage ("The Basement Concert Stage")

📷 [IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: assets/substage-cavern.jpg]

Situated forty feet directly underneath the West Wing, this forgotten stone cathedral features vaulted architectural arches, heavy acoustic damping panels, and amber fairy lights strung across vintage stage monitor stacks. Far from faculty rules and civilian eyes, all masks are stripped away here. As Girls of Phantom, the band plugs in weaponized instruments to ignite their familiar spirits and drive a 180 BPM tempo.

The unique subterranean acoustic geometry amplifies their music into tangible, color-coded Resonance Barriers that shatter real-world Dissonance static, forcing the girls to rely entirely on absolute emotional synchronization to survive high-tension breaches.

🏙️ Section 3: Around Town & Commercial Districts

After-School Life

Sakuraoka Shopping District

📷 [IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: assets/shopping-district.jpg]

The vibrant pedestrian avenue just outside campus gates, lined with crepe stands, independent music shops, and outdoor cafes under glowing neon signs. After grueling subterranean rehearsals, the girls decompress here—Akari inhaling strawberry parfaits in seconds, Aoi reviewing expense reports for guitar strings, and Sera and Nekomi sharing a quiet table.

This district is a core hub for slice-of-life romance and downtime, capturing quiet moments like Sera teasing Nekomi about getting whipped cream on her cheek, causing Nekomi to defensively look away into the neon glow while her familiar, Yozora, purrs from the shadows of a nearby bench.

High-Dissonance Zone

Shibuya Commercial Edge & Plazas

📷 [IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: assets/shibuya-mall.jpg]

A crowded labyrinth of multi-story indoor-outdoor mall plazas, rooftop walkways, and blazing neon arcade halls. Because it attracts heavy youth crowds dealing with intense academic pressure, social anxiety, and career uncertainty, Shibuya acts as a high-stakes hunting ground and primary hotbed for sudden, severe atmospheric static drops.

Here, the band operates like tactical covert agents: Aoi monitors digital billboard static from her smartphone, Akari scopes out multi-story arcade entrances, and Sera and Nekomi stake out rooftop observation points waiting for the exact moment to intervene.

🌌 Section 4: Town History & Valley Lore

Geographical History

Sprawling Skyview & Ancient Valley Lore

📷 [IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: assets/town-skyview.jpg]

Sakuraoka sits nestled in a natural geographical valley basin surrounded by hills that physically trap emotional "humidity"—accumulated stress, grief, and unspoken loneliness from generations past. Centuries ago, local shrine maidens utilized sacred acoustic bells to clear the valley's spiritual air.

Today, EchoHeart's modern incarnation carries that ancient torch forward, substituting temple bells with high-gain electric J-Rock, keytars, and custom amplification gear. Aoi maintains historical valley maps detailing these ancient acoustic resonance nodes, reminding the girls that their music is a vital safeguard keeping the entire town from fracturing under the weight of its own emotional history.
