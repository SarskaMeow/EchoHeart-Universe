EchoHeart — Chapter 1: Prep Room Harmonies

The lingering warmth of late afternoon filtered through the dusty blinds of Sakuraoka High's second-floor prep room, casting long golden slashes across the scuffed linoleum floor.

Nekomi tapped her fingernails against the edge of the worn wooden desk, keeping rhythm to a tempo only she could hear. Across the small room, tucked beside stacked audio monitors and coiled XLR cables, Sera adjusted the headphones resting around her neck.

"Are you sure about this key adjustment?" Sera asked softly, her thumb hovering over the synthesizer keyboard. "If we drop it half a step, the vocal harmonies in the chorus will intersect right where the acoustic resonance peaks."

Nekomi smiled, picking up her microphone. "That's exactly why we're doing it. Trust the beat, Sera. When the two voices blend, that's when the heartbeat starts."
