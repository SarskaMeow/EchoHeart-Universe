THE CORE 4 BAND MEMBERS

🎸 Nekomi Aoyama

Lead Guitarist & Main Vocalist
📷 [NEKOMI: School Uniform & Cardigan] ⚡ [NEKOMI: Dark Leather Jacket & Violet Glow]

A quiet cat girl who pours all her unspoken feelings into her guitar solos. She plays with absolute passion from the heart to reach anyone struggling in silence.

Instrument: Crescent Guitar Aura: Deep Violet Companion: Yozora (Black Cat)

⌨️ Sera Hanibara

Keyboardist & Harmony Singer
📷 [SERA: School Cardigan & Cat Clip] ⚡ [SERA: Rose-Gold Synth Outfit]

A gentle cat girl with pastel pink hair and a sweet, uplifting singing voice. Her bright synth chords bring warmth and comfort whenever the room feels heavy.

Instrument: Keytar & Synthesizer Aura: Warm Rose-Gold Companion: Byakko (Two-Tailed Fox)

🥁 Akari Minase

High-Energy Drummer
📷 [AKARI: School Vest & Ponytail] ⚡ [AKARI: Punk Drumming Vest]

Unstoppable and energetic, Akari is the emotional spark plug of the group. Her fast, powerful drumming drives the music forward and keeps everyone smiling.

Instrument: Custom Drum Set Aura: Vibrant Crimson Companion: Kagerou (Ember Marten)

🎸 Aoi Mizuhara

Bassist & Student Council Member
📷 [AOI: Sweater Vest & Glasses] ⚡ [AOI: Long Techwear Coat]

Calm, organized, and deeply thoughtful. Aoi manages club paperwork, keeps practice runs on schedule, and provides rich, grounding basslines for every song.

Instrument: Electric Bass Guitar Aura: Calm Teal Companion: Komorebi (Tanuki)
