OUR STORY & LOCATIONS

🌸 The private bond and secret music of Sakuraoka High

⚡ How a phantom band reached students who felt lost in the dark

THE DUAL IDENTITY

Campus Urban Legend

Girls Phantom Dead

To the students of Sakuraoka High, "Girls Phantom Dead" is a mysterious phantom band whispered about in hallway rumors. They drop black cardstock tickets stamped with a hollow heart into shoe lockers, inviting students dealing with heavy stress to secret underground performances.

Private Inner Core

EchoHeart

Behind closed doors in Music Prep Room B, "EchoHeart" is the true name of the four girls' connection. It represents their shared heartbeats bouncing back and forth—an echo of genuine friendship, warmth, and trust that holds Nekomi, Sera, Akari, and Aoi together.

KEY LOCATIONS

3rd Floor West Wing

Music Prep Room B

The quiet home base where the girls drink warm barley tea, write song lyrics, and plan their after-school rehearsals. A secret trapdoor hidden behind the piano leads down to their live performance space.

Underground Secret Venue

The Basement

A cavernous stone space directly beneath the music prep room. Away from teachers and campus noise, this is where the band plugs in their amplifiers and performs high-energy J-Rock sets to lift heavy burdens from fellow students.
