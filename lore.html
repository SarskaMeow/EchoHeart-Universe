Ancient Heritage

The Moonlit Feline Lineage

Long before Sakuraoka High was built, an ancient sanctuary stood on these grounds where spirit guardians bonded with chosen mortal bloodlines. Cat-girls like Nekomi and Sera carry this inherited resonance—granting them heightened intuitive perception for emotional frequencies, pitch perfection, and natural aura-weaving through musical chords.

Subterranean Origin

The Acoustic Cavern Beneath the Earth

The Basement isn't merely an empty cellar; it is a natural stone chamber carved over centuries. The stone acts as a natural acoustic amplifier that traps suppressed emotional energy from the school grounds above. When EchoHeart plays, the cavern converts those hidden burdens into resonant J-Rock waves that clear the heavy static from listeners' hearts.

Spirit Bond • Feline

Yozora & Byakko (The Moon & Dawn)

Yozora (bonded with Nekomi) is a crescent-marked black spirit cat who judges acoustic resonance and keeps the band's focus sharp. Byakko (bonded with Sera) is a two-tailed white spirit fox who embodies peace and warmth, often resting beneath Sera's keytar stand while tea steams nearby.

Spirit Bond • Earth & Ember

Kagerou & Komorebi (Ember & Earth)

Kagerou (bonded with Akari) is a crimson ember marten whose tail sparks in time with 180 BPM drum tempos. Komorebi (bonded with Aoi) is a moss-eared tanuki spirit who stays grounded beside Aoi's bass amplifier and paperwork stacks, ensuring the band's foundation never wavers.

Modern Beginning

The Meeting in Prep Room B

The modern chapter began on a rainy Tuesday when Nekomi sat alone in the abandoned 3rd Floor Music Prep Room tuning her Crescent guitar. Sera arrived with warm barley tea, Akari followed with her stick bag, and Aoi stepped in with student council papers. Their voices and instruments aligned instantly, unlocking the trapdoor behind the piano for the first time in decades.

Case Log #001 • Resolved

The Silent Paintbrush (Yumi Takahashi)

Yumi, a 2nd-year art student, lost her passion for painting after a gallery rejection. Nekomi dropped a shadow ticket stamped [ ♡ ] into locker 102. During a private performance in The Basement, the band played Crescent Resonance, rekindling her artistic spirit.

"Your paintbrush hasn't lost its voice. Come listen beneath the floorboards."
Case Log #002 • Resolved

The Stalled Runner (Ren Tachibana)

Ren, a track athlete, was paralyzed by doubt after an ankle injury. Akari matched his heartbeat with a fast-tempo drum drill as the band played at the stadium finish line as dusk settled, restoring his confidence to run again.

"Your legs aren't broken. Run toward the sound when the track goes dark."
Unspoken Protocol

The Three Laws of the Shadow Ticket

  • 1. No Public Sales: Shadow tickets can never be bought or sold. They are dropped into lockers only when true emotional distress is felt.
  • 2. Absolute Secrecy: The trapdoor location remains hidden from faculty and student council patrols.
  • 3. One Ticket, One Soul: Every concert setlist is customized specifically for the target student in need.
