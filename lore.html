LORE & WORLD MECHANICS

The dual identities, sanctuary domains, and acoustic physics governing Sakuraoka High and the subterranean vaults.

DUAL IDENTITY SYSTEM

To the students and faculty of Sakuraoka High, the girls are a sweet daytime Light Music Club. Beneath the surface, they are high-decibel acoustic vigilantes.

🌸 EchoHeart (Daytime Persona)

Primary Setting: Music Prep Room B & Campus Courtyard Stage

Genre & Style: J-Pop & K-Pop Cover Arrangements, Acoustic Demos, Festival Anthems

Acts as the public cover for the Core 4. EchoHeart presents an innocent, polished pop aesthetic that keeps Student Council oversight and faculty noise complaints at bay while maintaining high campus popularity.

⚡ Girls Phantom Dead (Underground Persona)

Primary Setting: Subterranean Sub-Stage Vaults 01–04

Genre & Style: 180+ BPM Heavy J-Rock, Post-Rock, High-Gain Distortion

The nocturnal vigilante identity deployed inside corrupted subterranean vaults. Armed with high-output amplifiers and mystical familiars, they engage acute emotional Dissonance through raw acoustic force.

ACOUSTIC PHYSICS: DISSONANCE & RESONANCE

🌀 Dissonance (Emotional Distortion)

Dissonance is formed when suppressed psychological trauma, anxiety, or perfectionism reaches critical mass within a student. It manifests as localized acoustic static, fog, or memory corruption within closed spatial pockets called Vaults. Left unchecked, Dissonance completely consumes the individual's emotional equilibrium.

✨ Resonance & Heart Sync

Resonance is the pure acoustic counter-frequency generated when band members align their emotional frequencies through music. When Nekomi Aoyama and Sera Hanibara achieve 100% Heart Sync, their combined lead guitar solos and Sun Voice harmonics trigger complete Dissonance purification.

THE 4 SANCTUARY DOMAINS

Each Core 4 band member anchors a distinct Sanctuary Domain corresponding to her element, instrument, and familiar.

Violet Starlight Domain 🎸 Nekomi Aoyama

Role: Frontline Offensive Core & Lead Solo Focus

Cuts through Dissonance barriers using high-gain Crescent-Moon guitar leads and raw distortion pulses backed by Yozora's shadow cat manifestation.

Rose-Gold Glow Domain ⌨️ Sera Hanibara

Role: Frequency Cleanser & Harmonic Shield

Uses the high-frequency Sun Voice and keytar synth pads to soothe acute psychological trauma, supported by Byakko's two-tailed fox light.

Crimson Spark Domain 🥁 Akari Minase

Role: Kinetic Energy & 180+ BPM Rhythm Engine

Provides physical impact through rapid double-kick drumming and stick-spinning fills, amplified by Kagerou's ember ember trails.

Mossy-Teal Wave Domain 🎸 Aoi Mizuhara

Role: Sub-Bass Containment & Tactical Stealth

Anchors low-frequency basslines that prevent subterranean acoustic leakage from reaching campus floors above, assisted by Komorebi.
