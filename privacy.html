Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

Welcome to Otaku Overload ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit otakuoverload.com.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Otaku Overload, we collect minimal information required to provide user accounts, live community chat, virtual currency tracking, and customized avatar experiences:

  • Account & Authentication Information: When you sign in using Google OAuth, Firebase Auth collects your Google Unique Identifier (UID), display name, email address, and profile picture URL.
  • Profile & Virtual Economy Data: We store your virtual currency balance (Orbs), equipped avatar frames, name glows, cosmetics inventory, and age verification status flags in Google Cloud Firestore.
  • Security & Bot Defense Data: We use Google reCAPTCHA Enterprise to detect automated bots and fraudulent traffic. This service may evaluate device software and network characteristics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information exclusively for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate your identity and maintain your persistent session across site visits.
  • To display your public display name, avatar, and equipped cosmetics in community chatrooms.
  • To track your earned and spent virtual Orbs.
  • To enforce age access boundaries (13+ Twilight Mode and 17+ Basement Mode).
  • To protect our infrastructure from automated spam, abuse, and security threats.

3. Third-Party Services

We rely on trusted enterprise infrastructure providers to operate the platform:

  • Firebase / Google Cloud Platform: Handles user authentication, database management (Firestore), and bot protection (reCAPTCHA Enterprise).
  • Advertising Networks (AdSense / Rewarded Ads): We may serve non-intrusive banner ads or optional rewarded video ads ("Watch for Orbs"). Third-party ad vendors may use cookies or device identifiers to serve ads based on prior visits to our website.

4. Children's Privacy & COPPA Compliance

Otaku Overload is strictly intended for individuals aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under 13 years of age in accordance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). If we learn that a user under 13 has created an account, we will immediately terminate the account and purge their profile data from our databases.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

Your profile data remains active for as long as your account exists. You may request full deletion of your account, stored profile documents, and associated chat history at any time by contacting us at support@otakuoverload.com.
